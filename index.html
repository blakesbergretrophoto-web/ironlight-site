How it works

  1. Discovery call

    Thirty minutes to talk through your story, who needs to see it and what you want it to do.

    You review a first cut, send notes and get final files in every size you need. On a brand refresh, we launch the new website and social profiles with you.

Questions

How much does a video cost?

Most single-day story films fall within the package prices above. The final number depends on the number of locations, interviews and deliverables. We'll give you a fixed quote after the discovery call. Working with a smaller budget? Tell us. We can trim deliverables or start with photo only and build from there.

Do you travel?

Yes. We're based in [City, State] and shoot anywhere in the U.S. Travel costs are quoted separately and kept lean.

How long until we get the final video?

You get a first cut in about [X] days and final files in about [2] weeks from the shoot day. Photos usually arrive within a week. Need it sooner for a launch or event? Tell us on the call and we'll plan around your date.

What do we need to prepare?

Not much. Pick the people to interview and give us access to the site. We handle the questions, the shot list and the schedule, and send you a one-page plan before the shoot.

Do you build the website yourselves?

Yes. We design and build it in-house around your new film and photos, or redesign your current site on the platform you already use. You get a site that loads fast and is easy to update.

What do we get for social?

New profile and cover images, banners and cover videos sized for each platform, plus a launch set of posts and reels cut from your shoot. Everything matches your website, so your brand looks the same wherever people find you.

Can we book photography without a video?

Yes. Headshot days, team photos and on-site photography are available on their own.

Who owns the footage?

You get full rights to use the final film and photos anywhere, forever. We ask to show the work in our portfolio, and if you'd rather we didn't, just say so.
